How to play

Assign every container to a qualified driver before the clock runs out

Pick a delivery note, read the container and cargo, then click a driver. Any driver who meets all the rules is a valid answer. Each level has an objective: meet it to complete the level.

Simulator rules

What the system checks

RuleHow to infer it
BaseThe driver starts from the delivery note's departure base (BCN or VLC).
SizeFirst ISO character: 2 = 20 ft, 4 = 40 ft, L = 45 ft.
REEFERGroup R in the code, or a temperature stated for the cargo.
ADRUN number and hazard class in the cargo (e.g. UN 1203, class 3).
INTLTruck destination outside Spain.
Hours9-hour shift. Service time = km ÷ 80 + 0.5 h loading and unloading + 0.75 h break if driving exceeds 4.5 h. A driver can chain several short services.
OVERNIGHTIf the service does not fit in 9 h, it needs a driver available for overnight trips and takes their whole shift.

These are simulation rules designed to train reasoning. They do not replace driving-time legislation (Regulation (EC) 561/2006) or the ADR agreement.

Tool

Decode the container's ISO code

From Intermediate onwards there are no requirement labels: the size and type code (ISO 6346) tells you the length and whether the container is refrigerated.

Careful: 45G1 is a 40 ft high cube, not a 45 ft container. The 45 ft is L5G1.

Advanced and Expert levels

Choose the customs procedure

SituationProcedureExample
Domestic movement, to another EU country, or goods arriving from the EUNONEBarcelona → Lyon
Goods leaving the EU (by road or loaded on a ship at the port)EX-ABarcelona → Andorra · factory → port for export to China
Non-EU goods released for free circulationIM-APort of Valencia → Mercavalencia
Non-EU goods travelling uncleared to a customs warehouseT1Port of Barcelona → PLAZA warehouse, Zaragoza

EX-A and IM-A are the Spanish Single Administrative Document (SAD) codes for export and import declarations; T1 is the external Union transit declaration. In real operations the customs representative or freight forwarder handles the procedure; here you identify which document the movement needs.

Scoring

How points work

PointsBase points × accuracy + time bonus − penalties
AccuracyCorrect assignments (and correct customs procedures, where they apply) ÷ total checks
Time bonusUp to 30 % of base points for time left, only with 60 % accuracy or more
PenaltiesRecommend aid −5 % of base points per use · Expert: −5 % for each high-priority note left unassigned while a low-priority one was served
Stars★ objective met · ★★ and 90 % accuracy · ★★★ 100 % with no aids
